How it works

Three steps. No downloads.

1

Chat on Kick

Watch any live streamer and chat like normal. Every message gives you a Kick Point for that streamer. Points roll over day to day, up to 1,000 each.

2

Type a word

Send one of the words below, on its own, in their chat. Big ones can be split: everyone who types the word chips in until it's paid.

3

Watch it hit

If they're in the city it happens to them live. If they're not, nothing is spent.
